Protected lesson videos

Upload each lesson directly to secure video hosting. Students receive a temporary playback token only after the server confirms their active course enrollment.

How to add a protected video

  1. Choose the course and module, then add the lesson title and video length.
  2. Select a video file (up to 200 MB). It uploads directly to Cloudflare Stream over a one-time upload link.
  3. Press “Upload & save secure lesson”. The video is then available in the learning room after processing.

Video links are signed, short-lived and restricted to the Englishmates site. This prevents normal link forwarding to another site. A viewer can still record a screen with another device, so no browser player can promise zero copying.

The secure video account must be connected to this Netlify preview before uploads can start. YouTube links are not accepted for paid lessons.
